Ideas

This page turns the catalogue into candidate secondary-analysis ideas. It is built around two core features: a Risk/Protective Factor Gap Matrix and a Research Question Generator. Together they identify studies that combine gambling measures with risk or protective factors, highlight under-studied domains, and suggest cross-study research questions that may be worth developing.

Research Question Generator

Questions are generated where multiple studies share a named gambling measure or a gambling harm construct and also contain a relevant risk/protective domain. The Existing evidence column cross-references publications in our catalogue that already address each question, helping to identify genuine research gaps.
